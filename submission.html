Submit Your Story

Before submitting, read these rules:

  • Only original stories or real-life accounts — no plagiarism.
  • Keep it within the eerie, twisted, horror, or obscure genre.
  • No hate speech, abusive content, or anything illegal.
  • Submissions can be factual or fictional, but must be clearly written.
  • Stories should be under 2000 words (for readability).
  • Mention your name and relevant details (age, alias, profession, location, etc.) if you want to be featured with proper credits.
  • You grant us permission to publish and slightly edit the story (if needed for clarity).
